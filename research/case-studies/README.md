# Case studies

These studies interpret first-party accounts through the Rebuild Labs model.
They demonstrate possible techniques and failure controls; they do not prove a
universal mode choice.

- [`react-fiber.md`](react-fiber.md): complete internal replacement with
  incremental development and rollout.
- [`github-blackbird.md`](github-blackbird.md): a constraint-led bounded
  subsystem rebuilt around code-search semantics and scale.
- [`stripe-subscriptions.md`](stripe-subscriptions.md): incremental online
  migration where data ownership and reconciliation dominate.
- [`gocd-branch-by-abstraction.md`](gocd-branch-by-abstraction.md): mainline
  architectural replacement through existing and created seams.
